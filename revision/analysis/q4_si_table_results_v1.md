## Integrated Case Study 1 consensus

All three final moderator syntheses linked (i) a hierarchical Mo/Ni-chalcogenide–rGO architecture that buffers strain and maintains electronic continuity, (ii) a sodium-focused ether/solvation-engineered electrolyte, and (iii) suppression of repeated SEI fracture, electrolyte/sodium consumption, and impedance growth through a thinner inorganic-rich interphase. The frozen 2-of-3 rule is therefore met (**3/3 high-level mechanism agreement**).

The agreement is mechanistic rather than compositional. Replicate 1 proposed an LHCE and a controlled-porosity 2×2 architecture–electrolyte test; replicate 2 proposed a DME-based electrolyte with a film-forming additive; replicate 3 proposed >2 M NaFSI in an ether and sodium alginate. Accordingly, the result supports a stable coupled design direction, not a uniquely recovered electrolyte recipe. Replicate 2's assumption that a film-forming additive will also suppress soluble chalcogenide intermediates and replicate 3's bundled electrolyte-plus-binder comparison require dedicated controls. The sodium-ion system was stated explicitly in all three runs, but each formulation remains a hypothesis requiring sodium full-cell validation.
