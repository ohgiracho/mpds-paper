# Four-condition component-control Pass 1 — case-level results

Date: 2026-09-20 (Asia/Seoul)  
Analysis unit: matched mean of three replicates within each of 10 cases  
Primary contrasts: DS-SA-IR and DS-SES  
Multiplicity: Holm correction over the five declared component-control contrasts within each metric

## Result that can be used cautiously in the revision

The component-control study supports a **specific interaction/integration effect**, not a broad claim that debate improves every quality dimension. DS exceeded SAIR on the IHQ cross-perspective-integration dimension by 1.53 points (0-5 scale; all 10 cases favored DS; exact sign-flip p=0.00195; Holm-adjusted Wilcoxon p=0.00977). DS did **not** show a statistically supported advantage over SAIR or SES on total IHQ after correction.

The added scientific/practical validity module points in the opposite direction: DS was lower than SAIR by 1.77 points and lower than SES by 1.27 points on the 0-20 descriptive composite. Both differences had bootstrap 95% intervals excluding zero and Holm-adjusted Wilcoxon p=0.0156. This is a reviewer-relevant limitation, not a result to conceal: the neutral debate configuration improves the integration-oriented rubric component relative to the single-agent revision control, but does not by itself optimize the separate validity composite.

## Length sensitivity

SAIR and SES responses were longer than DS on average. For full IHQ, DS-SA-IR had a mean output-length difference of -3,489 characters; the case-level difference-score/length-difference Spearman correlation was 0.60. The planned length-sensitivity file reports the case-level equal-length intercept and slope for every declared contrast and metric. These exploratory adjusted values should be reported as a sensitivity analysis, not substituted for the prespecified paired estimates above.

## Guardrails for manuscript language

- Do not state that MPDS or DS broadly outperformed every control.
- State that the neutral multi-agent interaction was associated with higher cross-perspective integration than the call-matched single-agent iterative revision control.
- State separately that the validity module did not favor DS over SAIR/SES, and frame this as a constraint that motivates the persona-conditioned MPDS design and the separate scientific-validity checks.
- Keep Raw/EO/EOP results in their existing five-condition analysis; they were not re-scored here.
