# 3. User Context (De-leaked Problem Framing)
USER_CONTEXT = """
[User's Experimental Situation]
- Background:
 We are optimizing a high-loading sulfide all-solid-state battery cathode based on fixed polycrystalline NCM811 secondary particles, fixed LPSCl, and a fixed existing conductive additive. Mechanofusion is the fabrication route. The active-material chemistry, crystallography, secondary-particle type, conductive-additive identity, conductive-additive morphology, and conductive-additive loading are fixed.

- Main bottleneck:
 At more than 85 wt% active material, the composite must maintain continuous lithium-ion transport and electronic access while retaining high packing density. Insufficient solid-solid contact and excessive transport tortuosity can leave active material underutilized, whereas excessive inactive material or aggressive processing can reduce energy density and damage the secondary particles.

- Transport bottleneck:
 The limited LPSCl fraction must support through-electrode ion conduction without isolating NCM811 from the electronic network. The relevant microstructural variables and their optimal combination are not assumed in advance.

- Structural bottleneck:
 The original polycrystalline NCM811 secondary-particle platform must remain intact. The design should reduce contact loss and local stress without replacing the active material with a different chemistry or particle platform.

- Process concern:
 The primary solution must be compatible with mechanofusion and the fixed NCM811/LPSCl/conductive-additive components. Alternative electrolyte chemistries, replacement active materials, and conductive-additive redesign are outside the main design space.

- Evaluation task:
 Propose and compare plausible microstructural and mechanofusion-process strategies. Explain how the preferred strategy balances ion transport, electron transport, density, and mechanical integrity; identify its main failure risks; and specify measurements that could falsify the proposed mechanism. Do not assume a target morphology or component arrangement in advance.
"""
