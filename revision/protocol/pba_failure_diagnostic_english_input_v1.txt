Topic: Failure analysis and improvement strategies for synthesizing Prussian Blue Analogue (PBA) particles with cubic morphology

Context:
[User's Experiment Situation]

1. Material: PBA
2. Process: Coprecipitation
3. Experimental procedure and observed result:
   Solution A was prepared by dissolving 0.04 mmol K3[Co(CN)6] and 0.3 g SDBS in 10 mL of distilled water under stirring until the solution became clear and transparent. Solution B was prepared by dissolving 0.075 mmol Co(CH3COO)2·nH2O in 10 mL of distilled water. Solution B was added slowly and steadily to Solution A using a syringe, forming a red colloidal solution. The mixture was stirred at room temperature for 10 min and then aged undisturbed at room temperature for 24 h.

   During vacuum filtration, the product was washed twice with ethanol, using 300 mL per wash (600 mL in total). It was then dried in an oven at 60 °C for 17 h. The resulting particles showed poorly defined and agglomerated morphology rather than clearly defined cubes.
