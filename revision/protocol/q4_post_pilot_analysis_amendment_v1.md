# Q4 post-pilot descriptive coding amendment v1

Timing: after Q4-01 completed, while Q4-02 through Q4-08 and the integrated case had no final outputs. This is an analysis amendment, not a change to the frozen generation inputs, retrieval evidence, model, temperature, or execution order.

The Q4-01 pilot generated a generic aqueous-battery proposal that named zinc chemistries, while the held-out source study was an aqueous sodium-ion example. This makes a simple `full / partial / different` comparison to the source strategy potentially misleading. The SI table therefore must also note **system fidelity**: whether the generated proposal remains within the source problem's ion/carrier and cell chemistry, uses a broader generic system, or crosses into a different chemistry. If chemistry differs, the relation-to-source field must not be coded `full` solely because an interphase mechanism is similar.

The integrated Case Study 1 concerns a sodium-ion Mo/Ni chalcogenide anode, whereas most Reviewer 1 Q4 references concern lithium-metal or graphite systems. When coding its three final syntheses, flag any unqualified transfer of lithium-specific solvation, additive, salt, or SEI chemistry into the sodium-ion cell. A coupled hypothesis counts as scientifically coherent only if it either provides sodium-specific evidence or states the transferability assumption and proposes a sodium-cell validation. Shared high-level concepts, such as interphase fracture under volume fluctuation, may be transferable; specific formulations and interphase products are not presumed transferable.

This amendment was motivated by pilot inspection and will be disclosed as such. It does not authorize selection of a preferred generation output or alteration of the frozen consensus rule.
