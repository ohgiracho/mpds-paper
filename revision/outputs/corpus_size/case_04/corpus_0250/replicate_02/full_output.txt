### Diagnosis of the Problem

2.  **Weak V₂O₃-CNT Anchoring:** The primary failure mode in such composites is the mechanical and electrical detachment of the active material from the conductive scaffold during cycling. 3.  **Lack of Microsphere Robustness:** A microsphere made of tangled CNTs is mechanically weak. During slurry preparation, electrode calendering, and electrochemical cycling, these fragile spheres can collapse, destroying the porous network you intended to create and increasing charge transfer resistance.
