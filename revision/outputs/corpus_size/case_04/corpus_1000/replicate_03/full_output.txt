**Proposed Solution: Sacrificial Template-Assisted Spray Pyrolysis**

    *   Burn out the PS/PMMA nanobeads, leaving behind a uniform distribution of well-defined macrovoids throughout the microsphere structure [ID: 13].
    *   Densify the CNT-V2O3 structure into robust, mechanically stable microspheres [ID: 1].
