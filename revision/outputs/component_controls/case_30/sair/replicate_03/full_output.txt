### A Recommended Framework: Bimetallic Selenide Yolk@Void@Dual N-Doped Carbon Shell

This design integrates bimetallic selenide nanoparticles (the "yolk") within a dual-layer nitrogen-doped carbon (NDC) shell, separated by an engineered void.
