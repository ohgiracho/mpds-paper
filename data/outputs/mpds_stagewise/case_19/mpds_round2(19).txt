### A More Practical and Controllable Path: Direct Selenization of Carbon Nanofiber Precursors

This is a highly scalable, industrial process.
2.  **Carbonization:** The mat is subjected to a two-step heat treatment: a low-temperature stabilization in air followed by high-temperature carbonization in an inert atmosphere. This converts the PAN into a robust, interconnected network of N-doped carbon nanofibers with embedded iron oxide nanoparticles.
3.  **Direct Selenization to the Final Active Material:** The resulting FeₓOᵧ/Carbon nanofiber mat is selenized in a tube furnace under a controlled Se vapor atmosphere. This converts the iron oxide in-situ into the final, more conductive **FeSeₓ active material**.

*   **Strain Buffering:** The architecture provides multi-level strain management. The carbon nanofiber matrix acts as a flexible backbone, buffering the volume change of the embedded FeSeₓ nanoparticles. This is precisely the strategy that yields long cycle life in similar systems [ID: 113, 464].
*   **Charge Transport:** We create an integrated, one-dimensional conductive network. The carbonization of PAN yields a continuous carbon fiber network, providing an uninterrupted highway for electrons. The active material is formed *within* this conductive matrix, ensuring intimate contact and eliminating the need for binders.
*   **Conversion Capacity & Process Control:** By targeting FeSeₓ as the final material, we eliminate the inefficient and hazardous oxidation step. We leverage a material with higher intrinsic conductivity and proven electrochemical performance [ID: 134, 464].
