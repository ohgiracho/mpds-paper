A far more resilient and electrochemically sound design is a **hierarchical, yolk-shell architecture, specifically hollow FeSe2 nanoparticles encapsulated within a nitrogen-doped carbon shell, which are then interconnected within a 3D porous carbon framework.**

1.  **Superior Volume-Change Buffering:** Your "holey graphene" provides external void space, but my proposed architecture provides a dedicated, internal void space for each active particle. This yolk-shell design is the pinnacle of rational design for high-volume-expansion materials [ID: 8]. The carbon shell acts as a self-contained, elastic buffer box that accommodates the expansion and contraction of the FeSe2 core without transmitting mechanical stress to the surrounding conductive matrix [ID: 19]. This prevents both particle-level pulverization and electrode-level delamination far more effectively than fragile anchor points on a 2D sheet. This principle is well-established; for example, in hollow FeP@C composites, this encapsulation prevents the continual re-forming of the SEI on the active material itself, which is a key to long-term stability [ID: 107].
