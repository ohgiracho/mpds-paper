2.  **Synthesis via One-Pot Spray Drying and Annealing:** This process is far simpler and more industrially relevant.

This one step accomplishes three things simultaneously: it converts the precursors into the crystalline Mo/Ni chalcogenide phase, pyrolyzes the organic additive into a conductive carbon coating on the primary nanoparticles, and sinters the nanoparticles together to form the final porous microsphere. This "double carbon coating" strategy—where carbon exists as a coating and as a matrix from the binder—has been shown to be highly effective [ID: 20].
